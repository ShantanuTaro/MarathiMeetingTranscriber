4. Accuracy: a transcript here is a draft

Automatic speech recognition makes mistakes, and it makes them confidently. On far-field meeting-hall audio, on code-switched Marathi and English, and on overlapping speech, it makes a great many. The Software marks some of its uncertainty in the document, but an unmarked word is not a verified word , one of the supported backends reports no per-word confidence at all, so on that backend nothing can be marked and an empty review table means only that the check never ran.

Output from the Software is an unverified machine-generated draft. It is not a certified transcript, not a record of proceedings, and not evidence. Do not use it, without complete review and correction by a competent human, for any legal, judicial, regulatory, governmental, financial, medical, employment, disciplinary, journalistic or contractual purpose, or anywhere else an error would matter. Speaker labels are statistical guesses and may attribute a statement to the wrong person.
